# Quranic educational assistant — sub-brand rules

A separate product under the ZOOKA AI parent. It is not an agent skin and not a marketing variant: it is a
religious-education product, and the rules below exist because a wrong claim here is not a design defect.

## Name and mark

Working name **«زُكاء القرآن» / ZOOKA Quran**, always adjacent to the parent lockup at half its height, never
merged into one wordmark. The device is a closed book with an open page and a Z cut from the page edge, drawn
on the same 100-unit grid as the parent symbol so the two sit together. Palette is the parent navy with the
blue→purple gradient reduced to a single blue hairline; gold accents are **not** used, because gold reads as
ornamental-sacred and the product is a study tool, not a devotional object.

## The rule that governs every screen

Every piece of content carries exactly one of three labels, in the reader's language, in a fixed position and
in a fixed colour:

| Label | Meaning | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **مصدر موثّق / Verified source** | A verse, a hadith or a tafsir passage with its citation (surah:ayah, collection, edition) | ice on navy, with a small book glyph |
| **شرح مُولَّد / AI explanation** | Generated text, clearly the product's own wording | muted text, with a spark glyph and the model name |
| **غير مؤكد / Unverified** | Anything the product could not cite | warning colour, and it is never presented as fact |

Generation never quotes scripture from memory. If a verse is displayed, it comes from a stored, checked text
with its reference; if none is available, the product shows the explanation alone and says which part it could
not cite. No invented verse, no invented hadith, no invented chain, no invented scholar's opinion.

## Presentation rules

Respectful default: no mascot robot on scripture screens (the robot speaks elsewhere in the product, never as
an authority), no gamification of verses, no streak pressure on religious reading, no faces or depictions of
prophets or companions, calligraphic text set in a Quranic typeface at body size or larger with generous line
height, and audio recitation credited to its reciter.

Accessibility: Arabic-first RTL, no letter-spacing on Arabic, verse text at 18px minimum, and a high-contrast
mode that keeps the label colours intact.

## What this sub-brand does not claim

It does not claim scholarly endorsement, a specific madhhab, or completeness. It does not present itself as a
substitute for a teacher or a mufti. The footer states, in both languages, that AI explanations are the
product's own wording and may err, and that verified sources are cited so the reader can check them.
