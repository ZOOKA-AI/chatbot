# Social media branding and claims policy

Every template keeps a 6% safe margin, the lockup top-left (top-right in RTL), the slogan bottom-centre, and a
6px gradient rule on the bottom edge. Text stays inside the safe area so platform cropping cannot cut it.

## Claims policy — enforced in the artwork, not just stated

1. **No verification badge.** No tick, no shield, no "official" chip appears in any asset. A badge in artwork
   would assert a status the accounts do not have.
2. **No illustrative metrics.** Where a template would show a number, it carries a visible `DEMO` chip. Real
   figures are pasted in only from a live source at publish time.
3. **No integrations shown as connected** unless they are connected and tested. Provider logos appear only in
   "not yet connected" contexts with that wording.
4. **No capability claim** that has not passed testing — image and video generation in particular stay out of
   marketing artwork until a provider is configured and a job has actually completed.
5. **Only verified profile URLs** are printed on assets. Unverified handles are left off entirely.

## Accounts — status must be checked, never assumed

The repository and this document do not assert that any ZOOKA AI account exists on Instagram, Facebook,
LinkedIn, X, TikTok or YouTube. Each must be confirmed at publish time by opening the profile; a URL is printed
on an asset only after it resolves. Known-good publishing infrastructure from earlier work covers Instagram and
TikTok for the Royal Haroon brand, which is a different brand and must not be presented as ZOOKA AI's.
