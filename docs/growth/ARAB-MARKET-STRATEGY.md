# Arab Market Strategy — from a Gulf product to an Arabic knowledge platform

## The change in one paragraph

ZOOKA-AI currently behaves like a Gulf SaaS with an Arabic homepage: 104 of its
last 28 days of clicks are UAE brand searches, and the Arabic homepage has never
been crawled. The strategy shifts from "market in the UAE and Saudi Arabia" to
"be discoverable across the Arabic web, monetise first in the Gulf". Organic
coverage is Arabic-wide from day one; paid spend and sales stay in the UAE and
Saudi Arabia until the funnel proves itself.

## Three engines, one loop

1. **Search** — a question is answered well in Arabic, on a page Google can crawl.
2. **Product** — inside the article, one honest path into a feature that works today.
3. **Distribution** — the article becomes a LinkedIn post, an X thread, a short
   video script, a Telegram summary, and a newsletter draft. Those channels send
   people back to the article, and the article sends them to the product.

The loop only closes if step 1 exists. Today it does not, which is why the plan
starts with crawling, not with writing.

## Why "all 22 countries" is a content decision, not a page decision

Arabic search behaviour is not uniform, but it is far more uniform than the
country boundaries suggest. A business owner in Amman and one in Casablanca ask
almost the same question about automating invoices. What differs is:

- vocabulary (منشأة vs شركة vs مؤسسة, أتمتة vs ميكنة),
- payment method, which affects conversion not ranking,
- occasionally a local regulation that we must not guess about.

So: **one strong Arabic article serves many countries.** A country page is created
only when we have something genuinely local: a verified local workflow, a verified
local payment or regulatory detail, or first-hand usage evidence.

## Commercial priority vs editorial priority

| Dimension | Order |
| --- | --- |
| Editorial coverage | All 22 Arab countries from day one, Arabic-first |
| Paid acquisition | UAE, then Saudi Arabia |
| Country-specific pages | Only where local evidence exists (see `22-COUNTRY-MATRIX.md`) |
| Language expansion | Arabic first; French tested with one page; English already exists |

## Positioning

ZOOKA-AI is not competing with ChatGPT on general intelligence. It competes on
**execution inside the user's own accounts**: Gmail, Drive, Calendar, GitHub,
Vercel, n8n. Every article must respect that line. Where ChatGPT is better, the
copy says so. That honesty is the differentiator and it is also what makes the
content trustworthy enough to rank.

## Non-negotiables carried from the owner's brief

- No mass-produced near-duplicate country pages.
- No invented statistics, expert names, customer stories or search volumes.
- No CTA pointing at a feature that does not work.
- No production deploy and no merge without owner approval.
- ZOOKA-AI and ROYAL HAROON never share accounts, assets, domains or audiences.

## What success looks like at 90 days

Not "52 articles published". The measurable outcomes, in order:

1. `/ar` and `/blog` are crawled and indexed (currently: unknown to Google).
2. Arabic impressions exist for at least 10 non-brand queries.
3. At least one article earns a click from a country outside the UAE.
4. Registration source is attributable, so we can say which article produced users.

If those four are true, the 90-day plan worked even with 20 articles instead of 52.
